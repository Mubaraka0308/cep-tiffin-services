Our Research Story

Built from Real Ground Research in Pune

As second-year engineering students, our Community Engagement Project (CEP) began by investigating daily food insecurity and meal dissatisfaction among hostel residents. Over 7 weeks, we conducted student surveys, tiffin provider surveys, and offline field visits to local kitchens in Sarita Vihar, Dhankawadi, and Katraj. We discovered that providers struggle with advertising and communication, while students struggle with hygiene trust and sudden provider closures. Khane ki Khoj was designed to bridge this gap.
